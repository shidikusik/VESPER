# VESPER artwork and audio provenance

All painting assets were generated for this project with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. They are used directly as runtime textures. No screenshots were synthesized: the supplied screenshots come from the running Godot scene.

- `assets/cathedral.png`: midnight blue underground cathedral, pointed arches, roots, mist, bioluminescent details; environment background without a character or interface.
- `assets/icon.png`: original moth sentinel, ivory teardrop mask, dark blue hood, short feather antennae, teal scarf, cyan sword; centered Android launcher composition.
- `assets/hero.png`: transparent full-body moth sentinel derived from the icon design, right-facing three-quarter stance.
- `assets/warden.png`: transparent full-body crowned warden, violet robes and plated armor, ornate gold crown, pale lavender sword.
- `assets/enemies.png`: transparent sheet with armored blue scarab at left and a broad-winged moth at right. Runtime texture regions select the two sprites.

The project uses one painted environment with blue, green and violet colour treatments, vector ledges and foliage, procedural motes, light halos, combat arcs, dash trails and animated sprite transforms.

Original ambient music and sound effects were synthesized with additive synthesis and envelopes. The ambient loop is 32 seconds long. The waveform files are shipped with the project.

There are no runtime AI requests, external assets loaded over the network, advertisements, or analytics.
